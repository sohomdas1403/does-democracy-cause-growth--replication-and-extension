# Democracy Does Cause Growth Extension & Replication Project

For "Foundations of Econometrics and Machine Learning", MSc 1st Year, Department of Economics, University of Lausanne

Extension of Acemoglu et al. (2019) “Democracy Does Cause Growth”: tests the IV exclusion restriction by adding lagged regional GDP as a control in both 2SLS stages to account for regional economic contagion. Includes global and regional estimates (LAC, EU, Africa, Asia, MENA, etc.) with first-stage diagnostics and long-run effects.
